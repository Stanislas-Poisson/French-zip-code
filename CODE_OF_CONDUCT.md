# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant][covenant], version 2.1.

In short: be respectful, welcome different points of view and accept constructive criticism. Harassment and abusive behaviour are not tolerated.

Instances of unacceptable behaviour may be reported to the project team at `contact@the-white-rabbits.fr`. All reports are reviewed and handled with discretion.

[covenant]: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/
